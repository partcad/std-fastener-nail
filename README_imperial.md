# //pub/std/fastener/nail/imperial

Imperial nails: the ASTM F1667 styles of timber construction - common, box, sinker, cooler, finishing, casing, gypsum wallboard, roofing and connector nails - by pennyweight and gauge.

The ASTM F1667 nails a timber frame, its sheathing, its trim and its roof
are put together with. Every one has the four ports and implements the
interfaces of [`//pub/std/fastener/nail`](README.md), which also says how
they are classified and what "outdoor" means.

Each part is `<family>/<size>`. The dimensions are fixed, as the standard
gives them; the shank, the point, the coating and the material are
parameters, defaulting to what the standard's table describes. A size the
tables do not have is `//pub/std/fastener/nail/metric:nail` with
dimensions of its own. Every size, with its dimensions, its point
interface and what EN 1995-1-1 asks of its penetration, is in
[CATALOGUE_imperial.md](CATALOGUE_imperial.md).
