# //pub/std/fastener/nail/metric

Metric nails: plain-shank wire nails in the sizes of DIN 1151, flat and countersunk, with the heads EN 10230-1 gives them, and a nail of any size as EN 14592 has one declared.

Metric nails for timber construction. Every one has the four ports and
implements the interfaces of [`//pub/std/fastener/nail`](README.md), which
also says how they are classified and what "outdoor" means.

## Any nail: `nail`

EN 14592 does not list sizes: a manufacturer declares a nail's diameter,
length and head, whether its shank is smooth or profiled, and its strength,
and EN 1995-1-1 designs with what was declared. `nail` is that declaration's
geometry - any diameter, any length, any head - by default a 3.1 x 90 mm
flat-headed framing nail, with a head twice the wire unless it says
otherwise:

```shell
pc info "//pub/std/fastener/nail/metric:nail;d=2.8,length=63,shank=ring"
```

Its shank is a parameter, so its grasp, `nail-grip;d=<d>`, is too, and it
names none in `connect.hold`, which takes no expressions: an assembly
holding one names it in `how.holdWith`.

## The DIN 1151 sizes

DIN 1151 named the classic construction nail by its wire and length - 3.1 x
80 - in two head forms, and its sizes are how these nails are still sold,
although EN 10230-1 has replaced it. Each part is `<form>/<d>x<l>`, in
millimetres. The dimensions are fixed; the shank, the point, the coating and
the material are parameters, and a flat head's length is measured from under
it, a countersunk one's overall.

The heads are EN 10230-1's (Table 1, for circular, flat, flat countersunk
and cup heads on a plain shank):

| d, mm | 1 | 1.2 | 1.4 | 1.6 | 1.8 | 2 | 2.2 | 2.4 | 2.7 | 3 | 3.4 | 3.8 | 4.2 | 4.6 | 5 | 5.5 | 6 | 7 | 8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| head, mm | 2.8 | 3.3 | 3.9 | 4 | 4.5 | 5 | 5.5 | 5.9 | 6.1 | 6.8 | 7.7 | 7.6 | 8.4 | 9.2 | 10 | 11 | 12 | 14 | 16 |

DIN 1151 has three wires that table does not - 2.5, 2.8 and 3.1 mm, where it
has 2.4, 2.7 and 3.0 - and their heads are interpolated in it; its 7.6 and
8.8 mm wires take the table's own rule from 3.8 mm up, twice the wire.

| Family | DIN 1151 | Head | Length | Sizes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `flat` | form A | flat | under the head | 28: `1.0x15` to `8.8x260` |
| `countersunk` | form B | countersunk | overall | 28: `1.0x15` to `8.8x260` |

Every size, with its head, where the head comes from, its point interface
and what EN 1995-1-1 asks of its penetration, is in
[CATALOGUE_metric.md](CATALOGUE_metric.md).


## Usage
```shell
pc list parts //pub/std/fastener/nail/metric
pc info //pub/std/fastener/nail/metric:flat/3.1x80
pc info "//pub/std/fastener/nail/metric:countersunk/3.4x90;shank=ring,coating=hot-dip-galvanized"
pc info "//pub/std/fastener/nail/metric:nail;d=2.8,length=63,shank=ring"
```
